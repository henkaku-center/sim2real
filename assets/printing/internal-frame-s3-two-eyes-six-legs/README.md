# Two eyes + six legs design variant

Prompts: “Give it two eyes”, then “Add two more legs”. Generated by the same
native V3 pipeline from `params.json` (the two-eye variant plus `middle_legs`).

- The two-eye widening (30 mm, cuts at Y = ±4.25 mm) is applied first.
- The frame and bottom cover are lengthened 52 mm at X = −3.5 mm, the only
  prismatic cut between the front and rear servo bays. Head and lid are
  lengthened 52 mm at X = 12 mm, which keeps the head's lower side cable
  notches their original size. Front and head screw axes stay matched.
- A middle station is the native front bay band (X 4–39.5 mm, |Y| > 31 mm before
  widening) copied 2.98 mm aft of the front bay's old position into the new
  midsection. Stations are 49.0 mm apart: rear −23.2, middle 25.8, front 74.8 mm.
- New parts to print and fit: a copy of each front hip and foot print per side
  (L5/L6, R5/R6), four more servos, leads on servo hub channels 8–11.

Findings: the battery cradle is now 52 mm longer as well as 30 mm wider; the
battery's factory leads must reach a power notch moved 52 mm forward; the
centre of mass moves aft relative to the leg span. Ribbon routes, leg sweeps
and physics are not checked here. Servo bays are compared with the source bays
(outside |Y| = 47.9 mm) with a 0.2 mm tolerance: Booleans across translation
seams can shave about 0.15 mm at a seam-adjacent corner.
