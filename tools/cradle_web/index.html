What this model does — and doesn’t

An open-ended tray with two low longitudinal rails, four external M3 mounting ears, and four through-base slots for two retaining straps. Straps are not modelled.

Sizing is nominal, not measured fit. The amber box is the battery envelope, not a detailed battery. Allow for pack variation, connectors, leads and your printing process.

M3 holes: 3.4 mm diameter. Mounting pitch follows the cradle dimensions; it is not a verified robot mounting pattern.

No electronics or charging system is included. Changing a battery changes balance. No battery purchase is assumed.
